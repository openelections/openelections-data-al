## Choctaw County — canvass OCR insufficient, no precinct CSV (2026-05-19 primary)

Choctaw has no precinct CSV and **none is produced** — the available canvass PDFs cannot be faithfully converted to per-precinct results via PaddleOCR, and per the no-manufacture directive a CSV of known-wrong values is not emitted. The certified `2026/20260519__al__primary__county.csv` remains the authority for Choctaw (15 offices, 62 candidate-totals across REP + DEM).

**Sources examined (both OCR'd with PaddleOCR):**
- `Chocraw County Blue Sheet and Canvas Report.pdf` → `.canvass_cache_paddleocr/Chocraw_County_Blue_Sheet_and_Canvas_Report/` (162 pp). Despite the "Blue Sheet" name this carries the **Democratic** per-precinct results (Will Boyd, Phillip Ensler, Darryl D. Perryman, Dakarai Larriett, etc.). 4 county-total summary pages ("WITH 34 OF 34 Precinct COUNTED"), 34 amendment-only pages, and 50 per-precinct office pages — all in the **separated** sub-format (candidate names with dot-leaders and no inline vote, followed by a separate vote block of `VOTE [PERCENT]` lines).
- `Choctaw.pdf` (Democratic Party Results) → `.canvass_cache_paddleocr/Choctaw/` (160 pp). Carries **both** parties' per-precinct results: ~6 DEM precincts in clean **inline** format (`NAME ..... VOTE PERCENT`) and the REP per-precinct pages in the separated format. Also contains a "Precinct Report" (separated) section and an "OFFICIAL REPORT" (inline) section concatenated.

**Root cause — zero-vote-line truncation breaks positional assignment.** In the separated format the vote block is a sequence of `VOTE [PERCENT]` lines, one per candidate in ballot order, which must be assigned positionally to the name block above. PaddleOCR **drops the vote lines for zero-vote candidates**, so the surviving vote lines no longer line up with the names. Worked example — precinct 0010 BOGUELOOSA REP Governor (`Choctaw/p045`), where McFeeters and Santivasci each got 0 and Tuberville got 120 (93.75% of 128 ballots):

```
GOVERNOR
KEN McFEETERS . . .
"Alabama" WILL SANTIVASCI . . .
THOMAS TUBERVILLE . . .
120 93.75            <- the ONLY vote line OCR captured (the 0-lines were dropped)
Over Votes . . .
Under Votes . . .
```

The real vote block is `[0, 0, 120, 0, 0]`; the OCR retained only `120 93.75`. Positional assignment maps that lone 120 to the first name (Ken McFeeters) — an **impossible** value (McFeeters' certified county-wide total is 77, so 120 in one precinct cannot be his; it is Tuberville's). This produces silently-wrong **over-counts**, not under-counts. The same mechanism shifts votes leftward in any office where leading candidates got 0 (the common case in small precincts).

**Sub-formats and why each fails:**
- **Interleaved separated** (votes follow each office's name block): the zero-line-truncation shift above. A ballot-count validation (reject an office when candidate-vote sum exceeds the precinct's ballots cast, parsed from the stats pages) removes *inter-office* bleeds (e.g. GILBERTOWN U.S. Senate summed 206 > 134 ballots → correctly rejected; ISNEY's all-9s garbage → rejected) but **cannot** catch *intra-office* shifts that preserve the sum — e.g. BOGUELOOSA Governor `[120,0,0]` sums to 120 ≤ 134 ballots yet is wrong (McFeeters 120 / Tuberville 0).
- **Consolidated** (all office name-blocks first, then one vote block at page end): the vote block mashes votes and percents from every office together with off-by-one column shifts (PELHAM precinct 2, `Choctaw/p013`, shows Tuberville as 0 and percents 50/10/20/10/50/50… spanning multiple offices) — unparseable.
- **Inline** (`NAME ..... VOTE PERCENT`, vote on the candidate's own line): the only sub-format with no positional-alignment risk, so values are OCR-faithful. But it covers only ~6 DEM + ~4 REP precincts (the DEM "OFFICIAL REPORT" section of `Choctaw.pdf`), far short of the 34 precincts needed; and even those pages are interleaved with separated/consolidated pages for the same precincts, so the max-vote dedup can still pull in a misaligned separated value (the BOGUELOOSA 120 above came from a separated page, not the incomplete inline one at p047).

**Percent-based candidate matching would not recover it faithfully.** A `VOTE PERCENT` line gives the vote *and* its share; matching that share to a candidate's county-wide profile (e.g. Tuberville 92% county-wide → `120 @93.75%` is Tuberville, not McFeeters 4.4%) can re-assign the dominant candidate but is **fuzzy** for minor candidates (a 10%-county-wide candidate may take 30% in one precinct) and risks manufacturing values rather than reading them. Per the no-manufacture directive this is not done.

**Outcome:** no precinct CSV is written for Choctaw. The `county.csv` certified totals stand. If a cleaner per-precinct source becomes available (a text-layer PDF, a re-OCR that preserves zero-vote lines, or the SoS machine-readable export), Choctaw can be rebuilt then. The two PaddleOCR caches and the emitter attempt (`/tmp/choctaw_emit2.py`) are retained for reference.

**Format — EL30 Precinct Report (inline):** the canvass flows continuously — precinct header → stats block → `***** (ALABAMA DEMOCRATIC/REPUBLICAN P) *****` → offices (each an office header + candidate rows) → other party → next precinct — and pages do not align to precinct/party/office boundaries, so context must be streamed across pages.
